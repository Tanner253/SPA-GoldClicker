Delete your Gold Clicker Mining account

This page is for Gold Clicker Mining on Google Play, by the developer of Gold Clicker Mining.

You can delete your account and its data at any time. You do not need to reinstall the game to ask.

Before you start

  • Deleting cannot be undone. Your progress, stats, items and ranks will be gone for good.
  • Purchases are not refunded by deleting. You lose anything you bought in the game. Google handles refunds. See Google Play refunds.

Option 1: in the game

  1. Open the game.
  2. Go to Settings, Account, Delete account.
  3. Read the summary and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away. Guest accounts are deleted the same way.

Option 2: without the game

Email goldclickermining@gmail.com with the subject Delete my account. To help us find your account, include one of these:

  • your player code, shown in the game under Settings, Account
  • the order number from a purchase, from your Google Play order history, which starts with GPA

We reply within 7 days and finish within 30 days. If we need to confirm the account is yours, we will tell you what we need. If you played as a guest and have neither of these, we cannot tell which account is yours. Inactive guest accounts are removed after 12 months anyway.

What gets deleted

  • your account and player ID, and the link to your Google Play Games player ID
  • your username, character and titles
  • your progress, balances, items and ranks
  • your place in your Town, and your leaderboard entries and ghost race replays
  • your stats, run summaries, run records and input logs
  • your ad reward records
  • your sign-in sessions, security results and device notification ID

Data held by other companies

We ask our service providers to delete data linked to your account. Some data is handled by other companies and is not tied to your game account.

  • Crash reports are kept by Firebase for 90 days, then removed.
  • Analytics data is kept by Google Analytics for 14 months.
  • Ad data is handled by Google. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.
  • Your Google Play Games profile belongs to your Google account. You can delete it in the Play Games app.

If you played the old version

The old version of the game ran on different servers. To ask about old data, email goldclickermining@gmail.com.
