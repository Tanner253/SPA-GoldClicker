Privacy Policy

Gold Clicker Mining. Last updated: November 2026.

This policy explains what information the Gold Clicker Mining game and the goldclickermining.com website handle, why, who else receives it, and the choices you have.

"We" means the developer of Gold Clicker Mining. You can reach us at goldclickermining@gmail.com.

The short version

  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, date of birth or exact location to play.
  • Your account is tied to a Google Play Games player ID, or to a random guest ID.
  • The game has no free-text chat. Other players see only the username you choose, your character, your Town, your game records and some of your stats. Your spending and ad stats are visible only to you.
  • The game shows ads. Google collects device and usage data to show them, and you can control personalized ads in the game's privacy options.
  • We never see your card details. Google Play handles every payment.
  • You can delete your account at any time, in the game or on the web. See Delete your account.
  • The game is not designed for children under 13.

Google Play may ask whether you want to share your age range with the game, and in some places the law requires it to be shared. We use it only to give you an age-appropriate game and to meet those laws. It stays on your device: we do not store it or send it to our server, and we do not use it for ads or analytics. If Google Play says your age must be confirmed, ads, data collection, purchases and sign-in wait until you confirm it in the Play Store. You can keep playing while you do.

Ads

The game shows rewarded ads that you choose to watch. It may also show an occasional full-screen ad between runs, never during a run. It does not show banner ads.

Ads may be personalized where the law allows it and you have not opted out. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your choice before ads load, and analytics and crash reports run only if you agree.

You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings, Privacy. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Some US state laws treat sharing data with ad partners as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. You can opt out in the game's privacy options.

How we use information, and our legal bases

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or a similar region, we rely on these legal bases.

  • To provide the game you asked for: your account, progress, purchases and rewards.
  • Our legitimate interests: keeping the game fair, and stopping cheating and abuse.
  • Your consent: personalized ads, analytics and crash reports.
  • Legal duties: tax and accounting records.

We do not sell game data. We do not use your Google Play Games data for advertising.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the game, under Settings, Account, Delete account, or by following the steps on our delete your account page. You do not need to reinstall the game to ask.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your information, to object to or limit how it is used, and to withdraw consent. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

To use these rights, email goldclickermining@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days. We may need to confirm the account is yours first, and you will not be treated differently for asking.

Children

Gold Clicker Mining is for players 13 and over. It is not designed for or directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game, email us and we will delete the account.

Where information is stored

Our server and database run in the United States. The companies listed above may process information in other countries, including the United States.

Security

Information travels over encrypted connections and is encrypted at rest. Sign-in credentials are stored as one-way hashes. No game ships with our server secrets. No system is perfectly secure, so please email us if you find a problem.

The old version of the game

This app used to be an earlier game that ran on different servers. The rebuilt game is a fresh start and does not use data from those servers. To ask about old data, email goldclickermining@gmail.com.

This website

goldclickermining.com has no trackers, ads or cookies of its own. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visitors' internet addresses for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes to this policy

If we make a meaningful change, we will update this page and its date, and tell you in the game before the change applies.
